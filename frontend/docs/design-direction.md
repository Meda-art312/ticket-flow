## SIGNATURE INTERACTION

Event art meets a perforated ticket edge. Opening an event turns the visual rhythm into a reservation summary; selecting a category updates the selected surface and keeps server prices visible. One brief route reveal, visible by default and disabled for reduced motion.

## CROSS-SURFACE REACH

Customer receipts and QR tickets keep the perforated divider. Organizer forms and sales tables inherit type, spacing, action colors and status language, with little ornament.

## QUALITY AND RISKS

Readable AA contrast, 44px targets, visible focus, accessible confirmation dialogs, bounded polling and truthful states are the finish bar. Risk: typographic artwork can overwhelm long titles; responsive wrapping and a maximum display size are required. No event photography was supplied; authored artwork must never be mistaken for a photograph or invented event data.
